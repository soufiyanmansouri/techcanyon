Finance AI is no longer a lab poster. It shows up in underwriting, financial crime, close, planning, and research. The hard part is picking tools that survive audit, integration, and model governance.

![Abstract AI and data visualization](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1677442136019-21780ecad995?auto=format&fit=crop&w=1400&q=80 "Finance AI now shows up in underwriting, crime, close, and research. Credit: Unsplash")

## Ranking snapshot (rewritten)

**10. Zest AI** — Burbank; CEO Mike de Vere. ML underwriting for affordability, access, and compliance-minded credit decisions.

![Mike de Vere, Zest AI](https://assets.bizclikmedia.net/1800/7a6394bcae56da6299a27765cdfbcfde:c2d717ef0e64b976360f191f72ac836b/mike-de-vere-zest-ai.jpg "Mike de Vere, CEO at Zest AI. Credit: Zest AI / source publication")

**9. Quantexa** — London; CEO Vishal Marria. Decision intelligence that links siloed data for crime, risk, and customer insight.

**8. Oracle Fusion Cloud Financials** — AI-assisted ERP finance: accounting, forecast, expense, risk; co-CEOs Clay Magouyrk and Mike Sicilia.

![Oracle CEOs](https://assets.bizclikmedia.net/1800/3cb529167ac143d70f80197efd7c3682:55d78fe464fe6dc9d3e758155d127840/clay-magouyrk-and-mike-sicilia.jpg "Clay Magouyrk and Mike Sicilia. Credit: Oracle / source publication")

**7. Feedzai** — Lisbon; CEO Nuno Sebastião. Real-time fraud and financial-crime ML with a focus on cutting false positives.

**6. IBM watsonx Orchestrate** — Workflow automation assistants for reporting, approvals, and data chores under Arvind Krishna.

![IBM](https://assets.bizclikmedia.net/1800/1ec6aaab61245435ed737f59ca96b53b:e0ac363d010b1136890a19022ab0e023/ibm.jpg "IBM. Credit: IBM / source publication")

**5. Upstart for Lenders** — San Mateo; CEO Dave Girouard. Alternative-data lending decisioning for banks and credit unions.

**4. Anaplan** — Miami; CEO Charlie Gottdiener. Connected planning across finance and operations with scenario modeling.

![Charlie Gottdiener, Anaplan](https://assets.bizclikmedia.net/1800/eb8c6674de5424d31c077a57bfd6eb10:a004ee760596b4694111b209a7aade58/charlie-gottdiener-anaplan.jpg "Charlie Gottdiener, CEO at Anaplan. Credit: Anaplan / source publication")

**3. BlackLine Verity AI** — Reconciliation and close automation with anomaly detection for faster, cleaner books.

**2. DataRobot** — Boston; CEO Debanjan Saha. Build/deploy/govern predictive models for fraud, credit, compliance, forecast.

![Debanjan Saha, DataRobot](https://assets.bizclikmedia.net/1800/70365a774120302ae59237c3de557800:3b969b248f67238631fc1dd682833a79/debanjan-saha-datarobot.jpg "Debanjan Saha, CEO at DataRobot. Credit: DataRobot / source publication")

**1. AlphaSense** — New York; CEO Jack Kokko. NLP market intelligence over filings, transcripts, research, and news — with gen-AI search/summary layers for investment and strategy teams.

## Buying checklist

Demand model monitoring, data residency answers, explainability for regulated decisions, and a clear owner for false-positive cost. “AI tool” is not a category — underwriting, crime, close, and research are different products wearing the same badge.
