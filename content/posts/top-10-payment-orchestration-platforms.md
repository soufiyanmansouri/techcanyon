Global checkout is no longer one processor and a hope. Merchants stitch together card schemes, local methods, fraud tools, and retries — then discover that routing itself is a product. Payment orchestration platforms sit above that mess: one integration, many rails, smarter path selection.

This TechCanyon briefing reframes a widely circulated industry ranking for operators who care about authorisation rates, cost-to-serve, and lock-in risk.

![Top 10 payment orchestration platforms](https://assets.bizclikmedia.net/1800/7f343e7def404400fc87d237ecbde489:1fa931e4981f403510dbf4e606f72da1/9-fintech-top10-graphics-cms-2026.jpg "In association with Primer. Credit: FinTech Magazine / Primer")

## Why orchestration exists

High-growth merchants face fragmented PSPs, alternative payment methods, and fraud stacks. Orchestration consolidates those into dynamic routing so traffic can chase better approval rates and lower fees without a rewrite every time a market opens.

## The field, ranked for context

**10. BR-DGE** — Founded 2018; CEO Thomas Gillan. Modular single-integration orchestration aimed at travel, retail, and digital merchants who want control without heavy engineering.

![Thomas Gillan, CEO of BR-DGE](https://assets.bizclikmedia.net/1800/b7481fcf310f9e5d297681f9f0da62fc:b7003863cf279d9ac1e8c441b6db906e/thomas-gillan-ceo-at-br-dge.jpg "Thomas Gillan, CEO of BR-DGE. Credit: BR-DGE")

**9. CellPoint** — Founded 2007; CEO Kevin Murphy. Travel and hospitality specialist linking 28+ card schemes, 168+ APMs, and 220+ processors with routing and performance analytics.

**8. ProcessOut** — Founded 2015; part of Checkout.com under Guillaume Pousaz. Orchestration layer with one API and routing tools for multi-processor stacks.

![Guillaume Pousaz](https://assets.bizclikmedia.net/1800/c6a9c157ee595ecceb1a1fa4eeae40d6:23797e8cd21b4d0eef990b2ced88b12a/64db80a5e88c6b1723ff7e97-1e137ed9-f874-472f-8b08-2aaf49ff046e-guillaume-pousaz-hero.webp "Guillaume Pousaz. Credit: ProcessOut")

**7. Yuno** — Founded 2022; CEO Juan Pablo Ortega. Global orchestration across 80+ countries and 1,000+ methods, with smart routing, fraud, and tokenisation; a16z-backed.

**6. IXOPAY** — Founded 2001; CEO Ajoy Krishnamoorthy. Enterprise orchestration with AI routing, decoupled tokenisation, and multi-acquirer compliance tooling.

**5. Primer** — Founded 2020; CEO Gabriel Le Roux. Unified payments infrastructure for growing enterprises; Accel, Balderton, Tencent, ICONIQ among backers.

![Gabriel Le Roux, CEO of Primer](https://assets.bizclikmedia.net/1800/d6c4adecf35cb78aa7953da5e88665f8:c9ad9db711b45d3c369dc2d1b54e4dfe/gabriel-le-roux-ceo-at-primer-credit-linkedin.jpg "Gabriel Le Roux, CEO of Primer. Credit: Gabriel Le Roux LinkedIn")

**4. Spreedly** — Founded 2007; CEO Justin Benson. Open vault/gateway fabric across 100+ countries and 140+ gateways; designed to reduce processor lock-in.

**3. CyberSource (Visa Acceptance Solutions)** — Founded 1994; under Visa CEO Ryan McInerney. Modular orchestration plus disputes, tokens, and AI fraud on Visa infrastructure.

![Ryan McInerney, CEO of Visa](https://assets.bizclikmedia.net/1800/6aed59c9d7e12aa23af86ee5d850d50f:87f1c76584e4425c1f47aae7754e1a13/ryan-mcinerney-ceo-of-visa-under-which-comes-cybersource-credit-linkedin.jpg "Ryan McInerney, CEO of Visa. Credit: Ryan McInerney LinkedIn")

**2. Stripe** — Founded 2009; CEO Patrick Collison. Full-stack processor that also ships vaulting and multi-processor routing for enterprises that need flexibility beyond a single acquirer.

![Patrick Collison, CEO at Stripe](https://assets.bizclikmedia.net/1800/31d3050503036ecb581451a86f4b9081:70a56ca9013f47eb22185c35ea49d02f/headshot-patrick-collison.jpg "Patrick Collison, CEO at Stripe. Credit: Patrick Collison LinkedIn")

**1. PayPal Enterprise Payments** — Built on Braintree under Enrique Lores. Combines native processing with third-party gateways, vaults, risk, and buyer methods (PayPal, Venmo, BNPL, local APMs).

## Operator takeaway

Ask who owns the vault, how retries are priced, what happens when a PSP fails, and whether “orchestration” is a true multi-processor fabric or a branded router inside one stack. Rankings are a starting map — contract terms and auth-rate math decide the winner.
