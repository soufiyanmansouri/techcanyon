P2P lending still sells a simple story: borrowers meet capital without a branch. Reality is risk grading, security packs, MiFID wrappers, and platform concentration. Below is a TechCanyon rewrite of a common Top 10 map spanning the US, UK, Ireland, and Europe.

![P2P lending platforms](https://assets.bizclikmedia.net/1800/e002e67c2d0c6fe972301ba4b327cd74:51976e450e1ea28dec994a189977f13c/unnamed-15.jpg "Peer-to-peer lending platforms. Credit: FinTech Magazine")

## The map

**10. Peerform (US)** — CEO Mikael Rapaport; near-prime personal loans via proprietary scoring; US$4k–25k, 36–60 month terms.

![Peerform](https://assets.bizclikmedia.net/1800/e3ed595869e62ede4bd892a79f2e458e:d5a854bb2f3bd02ec5dfcd367b8c2735/peerform-thumb.webp "Peerform. Credit: Peerform / source publication")

**9. Linked Finance (Ireland)** — CEO Niall O'Grady; SME marketplace; €10k–€500k; selective approvals (~25%); 6–11% expected returns; €250m+ funded.

![Linked Finance](https://assets.bizclikmedia.net/1800/750b73c9705eef8cad77ffee806da018:abdba8b9a9c7b22c15a76cdc8b9be50d/linked-finance-logo-1.jpg "Linked Finance. Credit: Linked Finance / source publication")

**8. Folk2Folk (UK)** — CEO Roy Warren; rural/regional business loans secured on UK property (~60% LTV); fixed ~8.75% model; £760m+ facilitated.

![Folk2Folk](https://assets.bizclikmedia.net/1800/2328ce0d43e5491306b132893dd97bc7:09f15642bb34297b2e9691ded01706e7/folk2folk-logo-1.jpg "Folk2Folk. Credit: Folk2Folk / source publication")

**7. Mintos (Latvia/EU)** — CEO Martins Sulte; multi-asset MiFID platform; loans plus bonds/ETFs path; 500k+ users; ~€611m AUA cited.

![Mintos](https://assets.bizclikmedia.net/1800/9e3b0ff298f7354d716f8f10fe6af974:9acc23717e97aee13418474386e32865/mintos-logo-1.jpg "Mintos. Credit: Mintos / source publication")

**6. October (France/EU)** — CEO Olivier Goy; pan-European SME credit; €30k–€5m; €840m+ to 3,300+ projects.

![October / Lendix](https://assets.bizclikmedia.net/1800/dfbf397b6a41d070d518326cfe56a73c:ac9ea6fae10bb749c0bdfbe9bccedf47/lendix-logo-1.jpg "October. Credit: October / source publication")

**5. Assetz Capital (UK)** — CEO Stuart Law; secured SME/property/renewables; target returns by risk sleeve; £1.5bn+ loans.

![Assetz Capital](https://assets.bizclikmedia.net/1800/47837d76eca41fad1bb8bf7c9f2be2e8:f7090225e717cbc98c4508aa362398c6/assetz-capital-logo-1.jpg "Assetz Capital. Credit: Assetz Capital / source publication")

**4. Kiva (global nonprofit)** — CEO Vishal Ghotge; from US$25 tickets; ~96.3% repayment narrative; US$2.1bn+ across 83 countries.

![Kiva](https://assets.bizclikmedia.net/1800/76144d1b35677ed664d9519b8363edf7:17f323453f58e13d15325ed2909e60c5/kiva-org-logo-1-1.jpg "Kiva. Credit: Kiva / source publication")

**3. PeerBerry (EU)** — CEO Arūnas Lekavičius; short-term consumer/business/real-estate notes; €2.89bn+ loans; buyback/partner guarantee framing.

![PeerBerry](https://assets.bizclikmedia.net/1800/7c5662d4f7854e6688daf7fb66969d2f:7c0f704755e694bc985c5708260a2bfb/peerberry-logo-1.jpg "PeerBerry. Credit: PeerBerry / source publication")

**2. Bondora (Estonia)** — CEO Pärtel Tomberg; cross-border consumer credit; Go & Grow automation (~6.75% target); €850m+ loans.

**1. Prosper Marketplace (US)** — CEO David Kimball; US pioneer (2005); US$2k–50k personal loans plus adjacent products; US$28bn+ originated; retail + institutional capital mix.

## Diligence notes

Secured vs unsecured, buyback promises, originator concentration, and published loan tape quality separate marketing from a portfolio. P2P is marketplace credit — price the platform risk as carefully as the borrower risk.
