FinTech & InsurTech Live’s second day — sponsored by Saphyre — mixed central-bank pragmatism, insurer M&A math, payments ecosystems, and partnership hygiene. Here is a TechCanyon rewrite of the themes that still travel.

![Conference stage and audience](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1540575467063-178a50c2df87?auto=format&fit=crop&w=1400&q=80 "A packed second day of panels on AI, partnerships, and trust. Credit: Unsplash")

## Main-stage signals

**Bank of England / Tangy Morgan** — Mentorship for startups sat beside a warning: AI and ML in payments and banking bring third-party concentration risk. The BoE push on digital skills and university partnerships was framed as infrastructure for safer adoption, not hype.

**AXA / Parul Kaul-Green** — Commercial insurance (~US$850bn market context; corporate risk ~US$350bn) continues to grow via M&A and tech. Coverage design still bends around country risk, floods, and supply disruption — software does not delete peril.

**Mastercard / Scott Abrahams** — Strategy stayed ecosystem-first: thousands of acquirer and issuer licensees. Build, buy, or partner decisions were judged by what strengthens that network, not vanity product launches.

![Event session photography](https://assets.bizclikmedia.net/1800/4390e0f22adc6d7962ffb6c56ae99316:e4a88147b17fa31c223c8ac9c1adde8d/image00007-jpeg.webp "FinTech & InsurTech Live session. Credit: FinTech Magazine / event photography")

**IBM / John Duigenan** — The blunt stat: most enterprise data sits unused (~74% cited). AI helps only after bias and trading-data quality problems are treated as first-class risks — experimentation without measurement ages poorly.

**Capgemini / Gareth Wilson** — Fintech leverage was quantified as faster product launch (~65%), more agility (~58%), and denser ecosystem collaboration (~53%) for banks that actually operationalise tools.

## Insurance CX and digital leadership

A CX panel (Insurance DataLab, Fairer Finance, Adiona, LV, others) focused on trust, what customers want, motor-risk fairness, and complaint handling. Marsh CEO Alistar Fraser argued digital-age leaders need agility, multi-level communication, decisive resilience — and cultures where disagreement is safe.

GBG’s Mark Mamone closed the trust thread: identity is holistic (more than name/DOB), onboarding and threat intel beat single-signal IP checks.

## Second stage: automation, embed, partner

Capto’s Marchela Georgieva and Alexis Chevallot framed pandemic-era digital acceleration and RPA as cost and quality tools — not job erasure. Leda Glyptis (10x) treated embedded finance as a collaboration sport. A closing partnerships panel (Capto, Capgemini, OakNorth, Checkout.com) circled trust, cultural fit, and complementary hard skills between banks and fintechs.

![Event photography](https://assets.bizclikmedia.net/1800/09c7267f0ae87143c50582c32d6a43c9:88583d067d5c00f8ffe086188175098e/image00002-jpeg.webp "FinTech & InsurTech Live. Credit: FinTech Magazine / event photography")

## Still useful years later

The through-line was not “more AI.” It was risk literacy, partner ecosystems, unused data, and CX trust — the unglamorous stack that decides whether demos survive production.
